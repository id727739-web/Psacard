About GradeVault

Consistent standards, transparent results

We grade trading cards the way collectors wish everyone did: consistently, transparently, and with a public record you can always check.

Our approach

Every card is evaluated on four factors — centering, corners, edges, and surface. At least two graders review each card independently, and disagreements go to a senior grader. Only when there's consensus do we assign a final grade and seal the card.

Each graded card is encapsulated in a tamper-evident case with a scannable label. The label ties back to a public certificate anyone can look up, so buyers and sellers share the same source of truth.

What we stand for

  • Consistency The same card gets the same grade, every time.
  • Transparency Public certificates and population data.
  • Security Insured shipping and tamper-evident cases.
  • Speed Clear turnaround times you can plan around.

Reference

The GradeVault 1–10 scale

A simplified overview. Half-point grades apply between whole grades where warranted.

10 · Gem Mint

Virtually flawless. Sharp corners, full gloss, near-perfect centering.

9 · Mint

Minor imperfection visible only on close inspection.

8 · Near Mint-Mint

Slight wax or print imperfections; strong eye appeal.

7 · Near Mint

Minor surface wear or slightly off-center.

5–6 · Excellent

Light rounding to corners and moderate wear.

1–4 · Good to VG

Noticeable wear, creasing, or edge damage.
